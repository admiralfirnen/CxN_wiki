Shadow Invasion

Official CxN clan strategy for the Shadow Invasion event in Total Battle. Follow these guidelines to maximize conquest points efficiently at the lowest cost.

Event Overview

Shadow Invasion is a two-day tournament event where you send your Hero plus three captains to attack the Shadow City and earn conquest points. As an Epic event, it is also one of the best ways to level up captains.

Key event rewards (personal goals)

  • Elite Summoning Scrolls
  • Scrolls of Triumph
  • Additional Conquest Points
  • Essence of the Immortals

Selecting Hero and Captains

The Hero does not have to be sent, but the event is a good opportunity to level them if needed. In general, the Hero should be lower level than your captains and only needs to be levelled enough to meet the prerequisites for the next Guardsman upgrade.

Maximizing conquest points

Send your strongest battle captains to the Shadow City. Good choices include Cleopatra, Aydae, or mounted/melee/ranged/flying battle captains. Use captains that provide strength bonuses; health bonuses do not matter against Epics.

Captains that do not help in this event

  • Dustan does not give a Conquest Points bonus in Shadow Invasion. This has been tested and confirmed in battle reports.
  • Amanitore does not help here: the event is not a clan march, reinforcements, or raid, so her bonuses do not apply.

Levelling captains

Send non-battle captains only when your goal is to level them. Best approach: send two strongest battle captains plus the one non-battle captain you want to level. You get more experience than by sending two non-battle captains and dealing less damage overall.

General Strategy

Troop stacking is the main way to attack Epics, including the Shadow City. All troops sent at an Epic will die. Epics have a 50/50 chance to hit first and will target the stack with the highest total health first. By stacking weaker troops (e.g. G1) in larger numbers, their combined health is higher than a smaller G2 stack, so the Epic hits them first. That lets your strongest troops get multiple hits in while weaker stacks absorb the Epic's hits.

This is the basic idea of troop stacking. Total health is affected by Hero talents, city research, clan research, equipment, artifacts, and captain bonuses.

Goal: deal the most damage for the lowest revival cost. Use Sacred Potions only for Mercenaries and Monsters. Use gold for Hero and captains. Delete low-level troop stacks and use gold only to revive your highest-level troops. Gold is the most precious currency—use it wisely.

Troop losses

When troops are killed, not all can be revived. In general, 10% are permanently lost and 90% can be revived. The game uses a rounding method that rounds up. In tests with stacks of 29, 30, and 31 troops, the 10% rule applied to the first two (3 troops lost each). For the third stack, 4 were lost (rounded up). When building stacks, use increments of 10 when possible, and avoid sending expensive troops in amounts just over a multiple of 10, as that can slightly increase permanent losses.

Kingdom Invasion scenario

If Clash for the Throne has Shadow Invasion starting on day two of the Clash, a kingdom with many high-level players can attack the Shadow City to gain Senate seats. The kingdom will need to keep growing its population and pushing players to higher levels so it can compete in this scenario and secure at least fifty Senate seats.
