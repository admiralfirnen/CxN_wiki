RED vs BLUE

Official CxN game rules and strategy for RED vs BLUE. The game is played in three rounds; each round is decided by three mini-games. Best two of three rounds wins the game.

Game Structure

The game is played in 3 rounds. The first round ends when your team has collected one-third of the resources needed to upgrade buildings for 100 members (at least 200 million of each: steel, concrete, planks, and religious tractates). The second round ends at two-thirds of each resource, and the third at 100%.

Best 2 of 3 rounds wins the game.

How You Win a Round

Each round consists of 3 mini-games. Best 2 of 3 mini-games wins that round.

You win a mini-game by collecting more of the relevant resource than the other team in that mini-game. The resource in question is either steel, concrete, or planks (as determined for that mini-game).

Resources

Steel, concrete, and planks are the three primary resources used to decide mini-games.

Religious tractates act as a wild card: the team may use them on a 1:1 basis as any other resource (steel, concrete, or planks). They are harder to obtain, but because they can be applied to any of the three, they have added strategic value. Use them to shore up the resource you need most in a given mini-game or round.
