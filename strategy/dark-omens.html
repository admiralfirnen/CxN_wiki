Dark Omens

Tournament overview + CxN clan execution plan

What is Dark Omens?

Dark Omens is a two-day Clan tournament where four Clans face off in the same division. Earn points, summon monsters, and suppress your rivals’ point production to climb the rankings.

How to participate

  • You must be in a player-created Clan that has a Clan Capital and 55+ members with level 20+ Heroes.
  • If you join an eligible Clan after the event starts, you can do missions and progress through the Battle Pass, but you cannot contribute Essence or summon/attack Dark Omen monsters.
  • Only available in Kingdoms with Cross-kingdom interactions enabled.

How to get Clan Rank Points

Clans generate rank points automatically. Contribute Omen Essence to raise your Flame level and speed up rank point production.

Essence contributions to the Clan Chalice are only available during scheduled time intervals. When an interval ends, ongoing production continues — only the ability to contribute is temporarily disabled.

Essence contribution windows (7 total)
  • Day 1 — +0h | 05:00–06:00 PM UTC
  • Day 1 — +8h | 01:00–02:00 AM UTC
  • Day 1 — +16h | 09:00–10:00 AM UTC
  • Day 1 — +22h | 03:00–06:00 PM UTC
  • Day 2 — +8h | 01:00–02:00 AM UTC
  • Day 2 — +16h | 09:00–10:00 AM UTC
  • Day 2 — +22h | 03:00–05:00 PM UTC

How to fight other Clans

  • Summon Dark Omen monsters onto rival Clans’ Grounds using Spawn Cards (from daily missions).
  • Monsters suppress point production (up to -95%) until defeated or they expire.
  • The limit on Dark Omen monsters refreshes every hour from tournament launch.

Suppression is capped at -95% — clans always produce points (just much slower).

Common questions (quick notes)

  • Spawn Cards: earned via daily missions by collecting Omen Sigils (from battles vs the Arcanomancer).
  • Dark Omen monster battles: reduced resurrection costs; irreversible losses disabled; equipment doesn’t break.
  • Fatigue: attacking Dark Omen monsters adds Fatigue Marks based on damage, reducing troop strength/health (encourages team play).
  • Matchmaking: based on clan might, member count, and the level of Guardsmen, Specialists, and Monsters.

Source (reused/adapted): Dark Omens — Total Battle Help Center.

‘Sandbag + Max Burn’

The basis of this strategy is to attract as many monsters for Spoils of Dread clan rewards as possible. So far there has never been an issue winning Omens outright, but rather maximizing total value.

Hit order (honor system)

If this is done properly and respected by ALL clan members, almost everyone should be able to get their Spoils of Dread chests. If people jump ahead in line, very few people may hit their goals and both individuals and the clan will suffer.

  1. G3/G4/G5 hit first. Once the red chest rewards are completed, no more hits on summoned monsters are allowed.
  2. G6 hit next. Once the red chest rewards are completed, no more hits on summoned monsters are allowed.
  3. G7 hit next. Once the red chest rewards are completed, no more hits on summoned monsters are allowed.
  4. G8, same.
  5. G9, same.

Arcanomancer expectations

All clan members are expected to attain maximum cards and essence from the Epic both days. From now on, the session leader will assume you have full essence and cards and simply check off your contributions after you have been called.

Strategy overview (session-led)

Do not play cards or contribute essence without session leader instruction.

The session leader will call for all contributions directly. Taking actions early compromises the plan and reduces the number of monsters summoned against us.

There are 7 Essence contribution windows. For the first four, the session leader attempts to stay slightly behind/even/slightly ahead of the leading opponent’s score. This focuses attention on us, resulting in more monsters summoned against us.

The last three windows are the maximum burn where we push the Chalice to level 5 right away.

  • Session leaders will ask which windows people will be active so everyone’s essence and cards can be used efficiently.
  • If you can’t be on during a contribution window, tell session leaders so they can instruct you when to use cards.
  • After the start of session 5, anyone who is very unsure of their schedule may summon or contribute essence at any time.

This is a clan event: follow session leader instructions and do not take actions with essence/cards without direct instruction.
