Health, blocks, and march order

Going after heroics is a health game: stack health boosts everywhere they apply. Run your Premium set to Great Marshal, use a health-focused captain that matches the troop types you send, equip a health-focused artifact aligned with those same types, and layer in your personal health bonus plus clan health bonus so the march can absorb return damage. Send large blocks of troops—big enough that each block can wipe enemy monster groups in one hit when possible, so you are not trading inefficient partial rounds.

March order: when you expect to die (for example, against an Epic Monster), lead with your weakest troops so your best stacks survive longer. When you expect to win (for example, against Heroic Monsters), lead with your strongest troops in those large front blocks so the fight starts in your favor.

Mercenaries and monsters

The types called out in the chart apply to mercenaries and monsters as well as normal troops—you are matching type strengths to the encounter, not only city units. You can also send none-type mercs when you have them; good examples include Epic Monster Hunters, Elder Hunter (Easter Bunny mercs), and Elder Bounty Hunter (Great Hunt mercs). Mix those in when they fit your plan.
